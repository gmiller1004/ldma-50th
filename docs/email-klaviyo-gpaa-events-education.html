Four ways to learn

Pick your method.
We’ll put you on the gold.

 

01

Metal Detecting

Ground balance, sensitivity, sweep speed, and how to trust a faint signal in mineralized ground. The pros walk the line with you, then turn you loose on private ground.

 

02

Dry Washing

No water? No problem. Learn how desert miners recover gold with air and vibration—all the way up to big multi-box machines like The Beast and Big Bertha.

 

03

High Banking

Classify, feed, and run paydirt through a high banker—then learn the cleanup that turns a sluice full of black sand into gold in your pan.

 

04

Dredging

Get in the water and see how a dredge moves material—and where the gold settles. The most hands-on way to work a creek, at camps where it’s allowed.

Yes—you’ll find gold.

This isn’t a classroom. LDMA prospects, digs, and stockpiles gold-bearing paydirt before the event, so the gold is there when you are. Run your own equipment and keep what you recover, or join a common dig where every participant shares in the gold.
